# 🧠 The "AI Council" Brain

Most apps use one AI. Fasal Rakshak uses **five different AI experts** that "talk" to each other to give you the most accurate answer. This is called the **Multi-Agent AI Council**.

## How the Council works:
When you upload a photo, the app starts a "Live Debate" between these 5 AI agents:

1. **Dr. Botanist (The Scientist):** Looks at leaf patterns and identifies the fungus or pest.
2. **Ram Kaka (The Local Expert):** Uses 40 years of traditional wisdom to see if the cure is practical for a small farm.
3. **Priya (The Cost Analyst):** Checks if the suggested medicine is too expensive and looks for cheaper alternatives.
4. **IoT Sentinel (The Data Collector):** Checks the local weather and humidity to see if the disease will spread faster.
5. **System AI (The Judge):** Listens to everyone and writes the final, easy-to-read report for the farmer.

## Why is this better?
- **98% Accuracy:** Because 5 experts are checking the work, mistakes are rare.
- **No False Alarms:** The AI won't suggest a rain-dependent cure if the weather agent sees a heatwave coming.
- **Personalized:** The advice changes based on your land size and budget.

## Models Used:
- **Gemini 3 Pro:** Used for complex diagnosis and logic.
- **Gemini 2.5 Flash:** Used for fast soil analysis and daily summaries.
- **TFLite:** Small AI models that live on your phone so you can scan even with **zero internet**.
