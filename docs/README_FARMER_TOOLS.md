# 🚜 Farmer Features & Services

Fasal Rakshak provides a "Complete Farming Ecosystem." Here is a list of everything a farmer can do in the app:

### 1. Dr. AI Diagnosis (The Scanner)
- **Image Scan:** Detects 500+ pests and diseases.
- **Video Scan:** Record a video of the field for a broad health check.
- **Voice Description:** Instead of typing, just speak to the app about the plant's symptoms.
- **Treatment Plan:** Get exact medicine names, dosages, and links to buy them nearby.

### 2. AI Market Intelligence
- **Live Mandi Rates:** Real-time prices from eNAM and local agents.
- **Selling Advisor:** AI tells you "Sell Now" or "Hold" based on price trends.
- **Quality Estimator:** Scan your grain/fruit, and AI predicts the price you should get based on its quality.
- **Digital Invoice:** Generate a professional receipt on your phone when you sell.

### 3. AI Crop Planner
- **Personalized Calendar:** A customized 120-day schedule from sowing to harvest.
- **Task Reminders:** Daily notifications for watering, weeding, and fertilizing.
- **Dealer Finder:** Automatically lists authorized pesticide and seed dealers near your farm.

### 4. Soil Lab
- **Visual Analysis:** Detects soil type and nutrient levels from a photo.
- **Improvement Plan:** Suggests exactly how much Urea, DAP, or Potash to add to fix your soil.

### 5. Transport & Logistics
- **Find Trucks:** Connect with verified mini-truck and tractor drivers.
- **Price Negotiation:** A built-in chat to agree on transport rates fairly.

### 6. Farmer Community & Chat
- **Farmer Feed:** Share photos of your success and ask questions to other farmers.
- **Krishi AI Assistant:** A 24/7 WhatsApp-style chat where you can ask any farming question.
