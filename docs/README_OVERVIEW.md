# 🌿 Fasal Rakshak: Your Digital AI Crop Doctor

## What is this app?
Fasal Rakshak (Protector of Crops) is a smart mobile application designed to help farmers grow healthy crops and make more money. Think of it as an expert scientist and a market specialist that lives inside your phone.

## How can it help you?
1. **Find Crop Diseases:** Take a photo of a sick leaf, and the AI tells you exactly what is wrong and how to fix it.
2. **Soil Health:** Scan your soil to see what fertilizers you need.
3. **Best Selling Prices:** See live prices from 500+ Mandis (markets) nearby so you never sell for a loss.
4. **Smart Planning:** Get a day-by-day calendar telling you when to water, fertilize, and harvest.
5. **Local Language:** The app speaks to you in 11 Indian languages (Hindi, Odia, Telugu, etc.).

## How to visit and use it?
- **Open the Link:** Simply visit the website link provided to you in any mobile browser (Chrome or Safari).
- **Install on Phone:** You don't need an App Store. When you open the site, tap "Add to Home Screen" to use it like a regular app, even when you have no internet!
- **Login:** Create an account with your phone number to save your farm history.
