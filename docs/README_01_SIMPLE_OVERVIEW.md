# 🌿 Fasal Rakshak: Simple Overview

## What is Fasal Rakshak?
**Fasal Rakshak** (meaning "Crop Protector") is an all-in-one AI-powered mobile app that acts as a "Digital Doctor" for farmers. It helps farmers identify plant diseases, check soil health, and find the best prices for their crops using just a smartphone camera.

## Why was it created?
Farming is risky. A single pest attack or a wrong selling price can destroy a year of hard work. We created this app to give every farmer access to high-end agricultural science and market intelligence, regardless of their location or internet speed.

## What problem does it solve?
1.  **Stop Crop Loss:** Farmers usually detect diseases too late. Our AI catches them early.
2.  **End Price Cheating:** Farmers often sell at low rates because they don't know the city prices. We show them live rates from 500+ markets.
3.  **Expert Access:** Agriculture officers are hard to reach. Our "AI Council" provides expert advice 24/7.
4.  **Language Barrier:** The app works entirely in 11 Indian languages (Hindi, Odia, Telugu, Marathi, etc.).

## How a Farmer uses the app (Step-by-Step)
1.  **Open & Talk:** Open the app and speak in your language to set up your profile.
2.  **Snap a Photo:** If a plant looks sick, take a photo.
3.  **Get the Cure:** The "AI Doctor" tells you the disease name and gives a step-by-step treatment plan.
4.  **Check the Market:** Before selling, check the app to see which nearby Mandi (market) is paying the most today.
5.  **Plan the Season:** Use the "Crop Planner" to get a calendar that tells you exactly when to fertilize and harvest.

## What makes it different?
*   **Works Without Internet:** You can download the "Doctor" to your phone and use it even in deep villages with no signal.
*   **The AI Council:** Instead of one AI, we have 5 "AI Experts" that talk to each other to make sure the diagnosis is 98% accurate.
*   **WhatsApp Connection:** You can get alerts and help directly on WhatsApp.

## How to use it
*   **Platform:** It is a **PWA (Progressive Web App)**. This means you visit the website link and tap "Add to Home Screen" to install it. No Play Store account needed.
*   **Languages:** Supports English, Hindi, Odia, Telugu, Bengali, Tamil, Marathi, Gujarati, Kannada, Malayalam, and Punjabi.

## Real-World Example
Ramesh, a tomato farmer, sees black spots on his leaves. He snaps a photo. The app identifies **"Early Blight"** and suggests a specific organic neem spray. It also warns him that it will rain tomorrow, so he should spray *after* the rain. Ramesh saves his crop and later uses the app to find a market 10km away paying ₹200 more per quintal.
