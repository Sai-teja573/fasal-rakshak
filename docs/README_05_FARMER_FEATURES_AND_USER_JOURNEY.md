# 🚜 README 05: Farmer Features and User Journey

## The Farmer's Journey

### Phase 1: Onboarding
*   **Step 1:** Farmer signs up using a phone number.
*   **Step 2:** **Language Selection:** Immediate switch to native script (e.g., Odia).
*   **Step 3:** **Farm Profile:** Farmer selects crops (Rice, Cotton) and land size. GPS auto-detects the village and district.
*   **Step 4:** **Offline Setup:** The app prompts the farmer to "Download Knowledge Pack" while on home WiFi so it works in the field.

### Phase 2: Daily Management (AgroHub)
*   The **AgroHub** dashboard shows a "Daily Insight" summary: "Good morning! Market prices for Rice are up. No rain expected, good day to fertilize."
*   **Weather Swipe:** Farmer can swipe left on the weather card to see soil history (last 7 days of moisture).

### Phase 3: The Crisis (Diagnosis)
*   The farmer notices yellow spots. They open the **Smart Scanner**.
*   **AI Guidance:** The scanner tells them "Move closer" or "Too dark" using real-time frame analysis.
*   **The Result:** AI shows the disease name in their local language. 
*   **Audio Report:** Farmer taps a button to listen to the report read aloud by Dr. AI.
*   **PDF Export:** Farmer generates a professional "Diagnosis Report" to show the local fertilizer shop owner.

### Phase 4: Selling (Market Intelligence)
*   Harvest is ready. Farmer opens **Market Intelligence**.
*   **Quality Check:** AI analyzes a photo of the grain and says "Premium Quality - You should get ₹2400/q."
*   **Mandi Comparison:** A table shows 5 nearby markets. Farmer sees that the market 15km away is paying ₹200 more than the local one.
*   **Booking Transport:** Farmer finds a driver, negotiates a fair price via chat, and tracks the truck's location.

### Phase 5: Community & Help
*   Farmer posts a photo of their successful harvest to the **Community Feed**.
*   **WhatsApp Bot:** Farmer sends a message to the Fasal Rakshak WhatsApp number to get a quick weather update without opening the app.

## Benefits in Daily Life
*   **Reduced Inputs:** Only buy the medicine you actually need.
*   **Higher Profits:** Sell at the right time at the right place.
*   **Peace of Mind:** Professional advice is always 5 seconds away.

## Future Roadmap (Planned Features)
*   **Drone Integration:** Uploading drone footage for whole-farm health maps.
*   **Blockchain Invoices:** Secure, tamper-proof sales records for bank loans.
*   **Group Buying:** Allowing farmers in a 5km radius to group-order fertilizers for bulk discounts.
